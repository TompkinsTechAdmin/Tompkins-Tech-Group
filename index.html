01 / About

Meet the person behind the work.

I’m an IT professional with nearly four years of experience after a career change from working as a Special Investigator contracted by the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency.

Today, I work as a Helpdesk Coordinator at M G Newell Corporation and hold CompTIA A+ and Network+ certifications. I’m especially interested in networking and systems administration across Windows and Linux.

Outside work, I’m a husband, father of two, gamer, and lifelong learner. I spend time working out, playing games, and experimenting in my home lab—often breaking something, figuring out why, and learning from it.

Tompkins Tech Group is where I document that work and help others with practical technology problems.
